Model-ready outputs

210 fields the pipeline extracts from a deal's raw documents and hands to your model. It does not build a model — every shop already has one, and theirs is the one their committee trusts. The work worth automating is filling it in correctly. Comps come only from an operator's own weekly rent scrape; licensed sources are excluded by design, so a field one of them would have filled reads MISSING rather than being sourced elsewhere.

What the public demo leaves to the internal pipeline

The demo is a deliberately reduced copy of the operator's pipeline: a licensed source, a portal login or a web lookup is not something a public page should reach. Each of these reads Available in the internal underwriting agent only wherever it appears, with the reason, instead of reading as a hole the pipeline failed to fill.

CapabilityWhy the demo does not produce itFields
School names and ratingsThe internal pipeline looks up the attendance-zone elementary, middle and high school and their public ratings; the demo reads a school name only where the memorandum states one and never rates it.summary.school_
Fair market valueThe internal pipeline pulls a market value by web lookup; the demo does not reach the web.summary.fair_market_value
Assessor parcel and billThe internal pipeline reads the parcel, tax district, assessed value, exemption and billed amount off the county assessor's portal; the demo shows the county's published rate only.summary.tax_parcel_id, summary.tax_area_code, summary.tax_billed_amount, summary.tax_billed_year, summary.assessed_value_basis, summary.tax_exemption
Address resolutionThe internal pipeline geocodes the address and records how it resolved; the demo takes the address as the memorandum states it.summary.address_provenance
Lease trade-outThe internal pipeline reads a lease trade-out report (new versus renewal rents); the demo does not ask for one.trade_out.
Licensed community unit countsA community's own unit count comes from a licensed data export, so the public demo holds it back; where a public record states a count (a county property record, a Florida DBPR apartment licence, a City of Austin permit), that count is shown with its source and as-of.comps.nearby[].units, comps.rows[].units
