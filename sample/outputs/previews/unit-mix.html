Per-plan rollup, in the rent-roll Summary's own columns. Market rent for model is the new move-in rent — residents whose move-in date falls inside the lookback window, whatever their lease type — where the plan has one, and the in-place average otherwise (in-place outright on a roll with no usable lease start or move-in date); the basis column says which, per plan. Leases signed is every classified lease started in the window, new and renewal together, shown for context and never fed to the model: a blended average is a renewal average wearing a market-rent label, and it understates trade-out on every deal it touches.
